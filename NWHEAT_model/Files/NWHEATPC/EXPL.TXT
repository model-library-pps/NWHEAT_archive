
The variables used in the 10 DAY OUTPUT have the following meaning:

CROP DATA
DAY= day number (1= January 1); 
WLV= weight green leaves (kg DM/ha);  
WST= weight of green stems (kg DM/ha); 
WRES= weight reserves in stems (kg DM/ha);
WSTOT= total weight in green and dead stems and stem reserves (kg DM/ha); 
WRT= weight living roots (kg DM/ha); 
WGR= weight grains (kg DM/ha); 
TAGB= weight total above-ground biomass (kg DM/ha); 
TB= total biomass with roots included (kg DM/ha); 
LAI= leaf area index (m2/m2); 
DVS= stage of phenological development (1= anthesis; 2=maturity); 
RD= rooting depth (cm); 
NPLV= N percentage in green leaves (100 * gr N/gr DM); 
NPLD= N percentage in dead leaves; 
NPSTOT= N percentage in WSTOT; 
NPGR= N percentage in grains;
NUPTA= N taken up (inclusive init.N in seeds) in above-ground biomass (kg N/ha) ; 
NUPTT= N taken up in total biomass (kg N/ha); 
RED= reduction photosynthesis by water stress (-); 
PARAB= total intercepted PAR (MJ/m2); 
PAR= total incoming PAR (MJ/m2)

SOIL NITROGEN
DAY= day number; 
ANL1 to ANL8= amount of mineral N in soil layer 1 to 8 (kg N/ha);
NTOT= amount of mineral nitrogen in maximum root zone , i.e. 10 layers 
    (kg N/ha); 
NDF= rate of nitrogen uptake by diffusion (kg N/ha/d); 
NUPTR= rate of total nitrogen uptake (by both massflow and diffusion); 
TNUPT= total N uptake in crop (kg N/ha);
MINOM= N mineralized from old organic matter (kg N/ha); 
MINFM= N mineralized from fresh organic matter (kg N/ha); 
NBIOM= total N immobilized in microbial biomass (kg N/ha);
NIMMO= mineral N lost by immobilization in microbial biomass (kg N/ha);
NFM= total N in fresh organic matter (kg N/ha); 
NLEACH= N lost by leaching below maximum root zone (kg N/ha); 
NSAM1 to NSAM3= amount of mineral N in soil layers 1 to 4 (NSAM1), 
    5 to 7, and 8 to 10 (kg N/ha); 
NRAIN= N in rainfall (kg N/ha);
NGIFT= N applied as fertilizer (kg N/ha).

SOIL WATER
DAY= day number; 
WCACT1 to WCACT10= actual water content in 10 soil layers (cm3/cm3); 
TTRANS= total transpiration (cm); 
TDRAIN= total water loss by drainage below maximum root zone (cm); 
TRAIN= total precipitation (cm); 
TESOIL= total soil evaporation (cm); 
WTOT= total amount of water in maximum root zone (cm); 
WAVT= total amount of water available in maximum root zone (cm); 
WAVR= total amount of water available in actual root zone (cm).


The variables used in the SUMMARY OUTPUT and the YIELD STATISTICS have the
following meaning:

Year= year number; 
SOW= date of sowing; 
EMERG= date of emrgence; 
ANTH= date of anthesis; 
HALT= date of maturity; 
WRT= weight of living and dead roots (kg DM/ha); 
WLV= weight of green and dead leaves (kg DM/ha); 
WST= weight of green and dead stems and stem reserves (kg DM/ha); 
WGR= weight of grains (kg DM/ha);
TAGB= total above-ground biomass (kg DM/ha); 
HI= harvest index (kg grains / kg above-ground biomass); 
LAIM= maximum leaf area index (m2/m2);
PARAB= total intercepted PAR (MJ/m2);
RUE= radiation use efficiency (g DM (total above ground biomass) / MJ 
    intercepted PAR );
DWAV= change in water available in maximum root zone (mm);
RAIN= total precipitation (mm);
ESOIL= total soil evaporation (mm);
TRANS= total crop transpiration (mm);
DRAIN= total loss of water by drainage below maximum root zone (mm); 
WUE= water use efficiency (g DM total above ground / kg water lost by 
    evaporation and transpiration);
MINOM= N mineralized from old organic matter (kg N/ha);
MINFM= N mineralized from fresh organic matter (kg N/ha);
DNTOT= change in amount of mineral N in maximum root zone (kg N/ha);
NRAIN= N in precipitation (kg N/ha);
NGIFT= N applied as fertilizer (kg N/ha);
NUPT= N taken up (inclusive init.N in seeds) in above-ground biomass (kg N/ha); 
NLEACH= N lost by leaching below maximum root zone (kg N/ha);
NIMMO= soil mineral N lost by immobilization in microbial biomass;
NUE= nitrogen use efficiency (kg total above ground biomass / kg N
    in total above ground plant); 
DNFM= change in total N in fresh organic matter (kg N/ha);
NBIOM= total N immobilized in microbial biomass (kg N/ha).
